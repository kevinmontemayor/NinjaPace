# NinjaPace Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 9, 2026**

This policy applies to NinjaPace for iPhone and Apple Watch and its Mac companion, NinjaPaceMac (NinjaPace Relay), developed by Kevin Montemayor.

## Our approach

NinjaPace does not require an account. We do not receive or retain your workout or health data on developer-operated servers. The apps do not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services, and we do not sell your personal information or use health data for advertising or tracking.

## Workout and health information

With your permission, NinjaPace uses Apple's HealthKit to record workouts and read information needed for workout tracking, live metrics, and history recovery. Depending on the feature and permissions granted, this can include workout dates and duration, distance, heart rate, steps, and energy burned.

Workouts may be saved to Apple Health. NinjaPace also stores workout history and related information locally, such as mileage goals, completion status, training schedules, awards, and preferences. The paired iPhone and Apple Watch exchange information needed to keep workout features synchronized.

Health information is used for the fitness features you choose. Recover History reads eligible NinjaPace workouts from Apple Health and rebuilds available local history; it does not delete or modify the original Health records. Recovery does not guarantee restoration of every setting or historical goal.

Apple's storage, synchronization, and device-backup services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. NinjaPace does not operate its own cloud backup service.

## Optional Mac relay and streaming

If you enable relay features, NinjaPace sends workout metrics to the relay address you select, ordinarily your Mac on your local network. The Mac companion displays the metrics and makes a browser HUD available for streaming software such as OBS. Relay addresses and display preferences may be stored on your devices.

This data does not pass through a developer-operated telemetry service. If you record or broadcast the HUD, the visible workout information may be included in the recording or shared with your audience and streaming provider. Those recordings and services are under your control and subject to the provider's practices. Use a trusted relay destination and network, and stop the relay or broadcast when you no longer want to share metrics.

## Permissions and reminders

Health, local-network, and notification permissions support the features that use them. Goal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Where available, system authentication protects access to sensitive network settings; NinjaPace does not receive your Face ID or Touch ID biometric data.

You can change permissions in your device's Settings or Health app. Revoking a permission can limit the related feature and does not automatically delete previously stored information.

## Retention and deletion

Local history and preferences remain on your devices until removed through available app or system controls. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. Apple Health records are separate: deleting NinjaPace does not automatically delete those records. Manage or delete them in Apple Health.

We have no developer-hosted workout database or user account to delete. Recordings, broadcasts, backups, and information held by other services must be managed with those services.

## Support and this policy's hosting

If you contact us by email or GitHub, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond to and resolve your request and retain it as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not post health records, private network addresses, or other sensitive information. GitHub and your email provider process communications under their own policies. GitHub also hosts this policy and processes visits under its privacy policy; visiting this page is separate from using the NinjaPace apps.
